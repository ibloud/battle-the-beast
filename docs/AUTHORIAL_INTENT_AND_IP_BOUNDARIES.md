# Authorial Intent and IP Boundaries

## Purpose

This document records the creator's present explanation of the relationships among
the works. It is an interpretive and stewardship record, not a transfer of ownership
and not a substitute for legal clearance.

## The originating ecosystem

- **The RIM** is the genesis of *Urban Alien Adventures*. It establishes early
  questions about consciousness, ownership, colonialism, and self-governance.
- **The Legend of Lady Violet and Sebastien** begins the lineage later encountered
  in PIXIE and the Violet material.
- **Violet's Revenge** follows a contemporary Violet who discovers Lady Violet's
  story while searching for who she is and where she comes from. The inherited
  story moves from evidence and imagination into lived reality.
- **Battle the Beast** is a transformative, nonlinear narrative laboratory. Its
  reusable contribution to *Veiled Dominion* is limited to abstract design
  principles and testable player experiences identified in the lineage document.
- **Veiled Dominion** is the original, franchise-neutral game system in which those
  principles are expressed as current mechanics.
- **PIXIE** is both a narrative work and a political statement about attribution,
  copyright, public domain, institutional power, and the cultural treatment of the
  work and legacy of Pamela Colman Smith.

## Violet and Lady Violet

Violet does not begin with complete knowledge of a predetermined ancestry. She
discovers the story of Lady Violet as a person might investigate family, identity,
and origin. At some point in that inquiry, the story became—or becomes—real.
The uncertainty of that threshold is part of the design, not a continuity error.

## Sebastien and collaboration

Sebastien is intentionally unresolved. The character should not be completed as an
idealised projection of what a woman imagines a man to be. Proposed collaboration
with Ren is a request for an independent male reading and character analysis.

That request does **not** make Ren the model for Sebastien, a co-author, or a rights
holder. Any contribution becomes part of the project only under a written agreement
covering scope, credit, compensation, confidentiality, consent, and ownership.

## Influences are not ownership claims

John August's and Ryan Reynolds' *The Nines* is a formal and philosophical influence
on the ecosystem's nested realities and questions of authorship. It is not part of
the project's canon or owned IP.

References to *The Magicians*, its Syfy adaptation, songs, images, and other media
inside the historical archive remain references to third-party works. They must not
be transferred into *Veiled Dominion* as original assets, characters, dialogue,
worldbuilding, or market identity.

## Stewardship rule

For every artifact, preserve four separate fields:

1. **Source and date** — what existed, where, and when.
2. **Rights status** — original, licensed, public domain, quotation, or unresolved.
3. **Interpretive role** — influence, archive evidence, research material, or canon.
4. **Transfer decision** — excluded, abstracted, experimental, or accepted into a
   named current project through its authority process.

No discovery of thematic continuity automatically transfers copyright, authorship,
canon status, or commercial permission.
