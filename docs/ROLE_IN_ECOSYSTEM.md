# Battle the Beast — Role in the Ecosystem

## Classification

**Transformative fan archive · nonlinear narrative prototype · historical design provenance**

This repository preserves a nonlinear story experiment written in conversation with Syfy's *The Magicians*. It is not Veiled Dominion canon, a commercial adaptation license, or proof that later mechanics were already fully designed here.

## Contribution to the wider practice

The archive provides evidence of sustained work with fragmented identity, consequence across resets, ensemble interdependence, multimodal story navigation, repair instead of erasure, and audiences assembling a system from distributed evidence.

These patterns connect to the discovery method associated with *The RIM* and Urban Alien Adventures. They later provide design questions—not protected franchise content—for *Veiled Dominion*.

## Transfer boundary

Transferable: abstract principles, research questions, interaction and accessibility lessons, franchise-neutral playtest hypotheses, and evidence of creative practice.

Not transferable: *The Magicians* characters, settings, plots, dialogue, images, music, terminology, other third-party media, or any implication of affiliation or adaptation rights.

See [From Battle the Beast to Veiled Dominion](VEILED_DOMINION_LINEAGE.md) for the detailed crosswalk.
