# Benefits-Aware Production Protocol

## Scope

This public protocol supports accurate recordkeeping for a disabled founder building
a studio. It contains no personal benefit status, payment amount, address, medical
identifier, housing record, or legal conclusion.

Program rules vary. Decisions about compensation, entity structure, enrollment,
grants, investment, or relocation require individual advice from the relevant agency
and qualified benefits, tax, and legal professionals.

## Before monetisation

1. Obtain a written benefits analysis covering wage and self-employment scenarios.
2. Request the founder's Social Security work-incentive history, including Trial Work
   Period months and current work-incentive phase.
3. Identify the exact housing program attached to the founder's unit and obtain its
   written rules for self-employment, business assets, grants, student status, and
   reporting deadlines.
4. Determine whether a Plan to Achieve Self-Support, vocational-rehabilitation plan,
   Ticket to Work service, or other approved support applies.
5. Review the proposed entity, IP license, and compensation model with a
   benefits-aware accountant and attorney.

## Records maintained from the first commercial activity

- Date, task, project, and time spent by the founder
- Gross receipts by source
- Invoices and ordinary operating expenses
- Grants, loans, investment, gifts, reimbursements, and restricted funds separately
- Contractor scopes, invoices, tax forms, payments, and IP terms
- Employee payroll and time records where applicable
- Founder draws, wages, royalties, reimbursements, and distributions separately
- Donated services, free facilities, subsidised equipment, and unpaid assistance
- Reports submitted to agencies and the receipts or confirmations they issue

## Repository and privacy boundary

Public repositories may contain this protocol, public business plans, sanitized
budgets, provenance summaries, and open-source code. They must not contain benefit
letters, housing records, tax returns, bank statements, medical records, personal
identifiers, contributor tax forms, or confidential institutional correspondence.

## Compensation integrity

Collaborators may be paid for genuine work under written agreements and appropriate
tax classification. Compensation must reflect actual services, be supported by
invoices or payroll records, and include clear ownership and credit terms.

A nominal founder salary, retained company revenue, or payments redirected to others
must never be represented as automatically excluded income. Agencies may evaluate
the founder's services, control, business profit, resources, and other economic value
under the rules of the specific program.

## Change-control gate

Do not implement any of the following solely through a repository decision:

- Founder compensation or a `$1 salary`
- IP assignment to a company or institution
- Equity issuance or revenue sharing
- Acceptance of a grant, investment, or paid research appointment
- Full-time student enrollment
- Relocation or extended absence from subsidized housing

For each change, retain the written agency or professional analysis, effective date,
reporting deadline, and person responsible for reporting outside the public repo.
